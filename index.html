Import prices fall again in Oct. on stronger S. Korean won, lower oil prices
Import prices fell again in October driven by a stronger South Korean won and lower oil prices, central bank data showed Friday. The import price index dropped 1.8 percent on-month last month, reversing from a 1.9 percent gain in September, according to the preliminary data from the Bank of Korea (BOK). →
Samsung, LG Display bet on China's gaming OLED boom
Samsung Display and LG Display are stepping up efforts to expand their organic light-emitting diode panel business for premium gaming monitors in China, the world's largest gaming market. The two Korean display makers are winning orders from Chinese monitor brands with their advanced OLED technologies while turning to premium internet cafes and gaming hotels to reach local consumers. But the growing presence of Chinese panel makers in OLED production could eventually challenge their technological edge. →
